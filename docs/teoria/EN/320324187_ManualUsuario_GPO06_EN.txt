1. The environment and startup
This space environment lets you explore as Rek’Sai, use a ship, follow a racing circuit and observe
animated objects. It contains a base and three planetary zones: crystal-covered rock, forest and an ocean
with a wave. Third-person cameras follow the character or ship; free camera and orbit provide other
viewpoints.
Requirements: Windows x64 and a graphics driver supporting OpenGL 4.3. Open PROYECTO_GITHUB
and run Jugar.cmd or the root LabCGeIHC.exe. Keep Models, Textures, shaders and DLLs beside the
executable. Blender and Visual Studio are not required to play. The application finds assets from its own
location even when launched from another directory.
After loading, Rek’Sai appears on the base; a separate console may also appear. The clock is in the
upper-left corner; the lower HUD identifies mode, zone and state. F9 releases the mouse. A console or
running process alone does not establish that the game is visible: a scene like the following must appear.

2. Implemented controls
Controls were checked against Main.cpp, Input.cpp and Window::ManejaTeclado. Y, U, G and O from
earlier documents are not presented as controls for this version.
Key / action
Result and context
F1 / F2 / F3 / F4
On-foot planet / racing / flight / observation. These are experience modes.
F5 / F6 / F7 / F8
Forest / rock / ocean / space. These change the zone.
W A S D + mouse
Move and look. In racing, W/S accelerate/brake and A/D change lane.
Q / E
Descend / ascend in flight; E also interacts according to context.
J
Board or leave the ship when allowed by state and zone.
C
Toggle free camera.
Tab / mouse wheel
In F4: next target / distance.
1
Activate/reset the procedural rocket in space or forest D1. In F4, a rocket must be selected.
2 / 3
Toggle capsule door / satellite panels.
4 / 5
Fighter departure and return / hangar door; 5 operates while parked.
6 / 7
In rock: play / reset the keyframed rocket.
F / L / K
Flashlight / ship light / base spotlight.
F9 / Esc
Release or capture the cursor / close the application.
Mode, zone and view
F1-F4 do not mean first, second, third or fourth person. F5-F8 change location. C enables a free camera
and the wheel changes distance in F4; these view changes do not create a new game experience.

3. Suggested walkthrough
1) Inspect the base and HUD. 2) Press F6 for rock and F1 to walk as Rek’Sai; try WASD and mouse. 3)
Use 2 for the capsule door, 6 to play the staged rocket and 7 to reset it. 4) Press F5 for forest. 5) Press F2
for racing; W/S control speed and A/D change lane. 6) In F3, fly between planets. Approaching an
atmosphere changes zone; E is contextual and portals provide exits. In the ocean, the ship’s route follows
the wave toward its exit.
In F4, Tab cycles through available D1, hangar, rock, forest, satellite and base-rocket targets. At the
hangar, 4 starts the Fighter departure/return and 5 demonstrates the door. At the satellite, 3 folds the
panels. F1 returns to on-foot travel on the last planet. F8 returns to space.
Figure 2. Hangar viewed in F4.
Troubleshooting
If no window appears, check the complete DLL and asset set and inspect the --probar-release log; a
dependency failure can terminate before any text appears. Use F9 if the pointer is captured. If lost, use F8
or F6/F1. If a key is pressed during loading, wait and press it again. The first visit may load models.
Hardware without OpenGL 4.3 is not supported.

Teamwork and my contribution
This project was developed as a team during the laboratory course. Each member worked on a different
part, and we then integrated those parts into one application. This documentation is my individual Theory
submission, identified by 320324187 and GPO06.

What I worked on
My main contribution was Rek’Sai: preparing the parts and pivots, adjusting the gait and connecting
movement to the controls. I also worked on keyboard and mouse input and the HUD, which displays the
mode, zone and player state. During integration, I checked that my contribution worked with the team’s
cameras and environments.
This contribution can be followed in src/game/avatar/Avatar.cpp, Input.cpp and src/ui/Hud.cpp, together
with their integration in Main.cpp. The history also records the later integration of TARS in the ocean.
Engine and world modules retain the work of A and B.
