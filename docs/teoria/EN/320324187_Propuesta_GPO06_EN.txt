1. Proposal and located source
Virtual environment for space travel and intergalactic species evolution. The located historical proposal is
a 14-page PDF whose cover states 30 September 2026 as its preparation date. It proposed two planets
and a space zone. The original remains unchanged in the original project. This is a Theory edition with
corrected identification and an updated description; it does not establish that the proposal was submitted
to Classroom before 20 October.
The product lets users control Rek’Sai and a ship across surfaces and space. It combines rock and
crystals, forest and an ocean wave with a base, track, capsules, satellite and hangar. Fictional-universe
references describe the academic prototype’s visual inspiration; they do not imply an official franchise
production.
Current scope checked against implementation
Four experiences remain: planet, racing, flight and observation. Worlds are separate states, entered
through proximity/interaction and left through portals or zone transitions. The avatar is animated through
parts. There are procedural rockets, a keyframed rocket, doors, panels and computed trajectories. TARS
is confirmed in Main.cpp and Tars.cpp. Gohan, domes and other planned models are not treated as
completed merely because they appear in the proposal.

animation, lighting and interaction within one scene. Submission claims must be supported by concrete
